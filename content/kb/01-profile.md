## What "full-stack" means for Kyan

For Kyan, full-stack means end to end. He takes a project from a blank folder
to a deployed, working product — architecture, data model, API, UI, and
deployment — rather than only front-end or only back-end work. He prefers to
own a project end to end instead of a narrow slice of it.

## How Kyan works: the five-step process

A typical engagement runs in five steps: (1) understand the goal and the users
— what the product is actually for; (2) architecture and data modelling, so the
schema and the system come first; (3) implementation of the front end and back
end; (4) deployment to a real, working environment; and (5) handover, so the
client can run and own what was built. He writes TypeScript and Laravel day to
day, leans on Tailwind CSS for UI, and uses AI tooling to accelerate research,
scaffolding and review while keeping engineering decisions and quality checks
his own.
