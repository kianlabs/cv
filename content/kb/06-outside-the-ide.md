# Outside the IDE — Kyan beyond the code

## Life outside the IDE
Outside of coding, Kyan is a maker at heart who likes to keep moving. He spends
his free time chasing new places, riding at night, and hanging out with his
cats — while helping run his parents' clothing business. That balance of craft,
travel and family keeps him grounded and curious. (hobi / hobbies, di luar
kerja / outside work, kehidupan pribadi / personal life)

## Travel — chasing new places
Kyan enjoys travel and exploring new places whenever he gets the chance.
Seeing different cities and landscapes is how he resets and finds fresh
perspective, and it often feeds the curiosity he brings back to his work.
New scenery, new ideas. (perjalanan / travel, jalan-jalan / going places,
liburan / vacation, explore / menjelajah)

## Riding — the Vespa at night
A night ride on his Vespa scooter is one of Kyan's favourite ways to unwind.
Riding after dark clears his head and gives him space to think — sometimes
about a stubborn bug, sometimes about nothing at all. It is a simple, honest
pleasure. (vespa / skuter / scooter, naik motor / riding, malam / night)

## Cats
Kyan shares his home with his cats and genuinely enjoys their company. They are
low-key companions while he works and a reliable source of calm after a long
build-and-deploy day. (kucing / cats / kucing peliharaan / pets)

## Helping run his parents' clothing business
Alongside his own work, Kyan helps run his parents' clothing business — a
family venture selling apparel. Supporting it keeps him close to family and
gives him first-hand insight into running a small business: customers, stock,
and the everyday realities behind the software he builds for others. (bisnis
pakaian orangtua / parents' clothing business, toko pakaian / clothing shop,
usaha keluarga / family business, jualan baju / selling clothes)

## How Kyan recharges
Kyan recharges by learning. When he is not building, he is usually reading,
tinkering, or picking up something new — then folding that knowledge straight
back into his projects. Curiosity is not a side hobby for him; it is part of
how he stays sharp as a developer. (belajar / learning, mengisi ulang energi /
recharge, rasa ingin tahu / curiosity)
